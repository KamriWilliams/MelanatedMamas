About Us

A village for mothers

Melanated Mamas is a nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting and empowering Black mothers throughout the Golden Crescent region.

We are rooted in inclusivity, diversity, and respect — and we promote the economic and social empowerment of Black women and their families.

Our Story

Melanated Mamas started when I saw a need that I felt called to fill. I prayed that God would help me build something that could be a resource and a friend to the women around me.

And that's exactly what it became — more than a group, it's a village in every sense of the word. Whether it's supporting a mom trying to get their teenager into a dream college, or being there for a mom struggling through postpartum. This is the place you turn to when you need support, a place to vent, or simply someone to chill with when the kids are away.

Our mission is simple: to support and empower mothers throughout the Golden Crescent region.

— Kamri Williams, Founder
